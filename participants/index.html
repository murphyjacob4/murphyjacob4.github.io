Valkey offering:

BetterDB is the first monitoring platform built specifically for Valkey. It persists the operational data Valkey exposes but doesn't retain - COMMANDLOG, per-slot metrics, per-thread I/O, and more - then actively analyzes client activity and ACL audit trails to surface anomalies, so teams can debug what happened at 3am, understand what's happening now, and forecast what will come next. It works both ways with OpenTelemetry, exports to Prometheus, and fires webhooks on anomalies - correlating your app's traces with live Valkey state. Migrate freely between Redis and Valkey, from self-hosted to cloud, or vice versa. Run it self-hosted, including fully air-gapped, or as managed cloud.

About the organization:

BetterDB Inc. is a public benefit corporation operating under the OCV Open Charter, building open observability, monitoring, and migration tooling for Valkey. The core is open source under the MIT license, with commercial features for teams that need enterprise-grade analytics, compliance, and support.

Freshworks authored valkey-glide-ruby (gem: valkey-rb), the official Ruby client for Valkey GLIDE, and contributed to the underlying GLIDE Rust core that powers it. Built on the same valkey-glide Rust FFI foundation as the Python, Java, Node.js, and Go clients, the Ruby client offers a redis-rb-compatible API, standalone and cluster modes, TLS/SSL, native OpenTelemetry tracing and metrics, client statistics, and comprehensive command coverage.
